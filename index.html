Who we are

A covenant people with a simple charge.

2023Incorporated on 3 April
9Aims written into our constitution
2Places we serve: Emiworo and Gboloko

We are a church family gathered around the Word of God and the person of Jesus Christ, known to many simply as Help for the Helpless.

Our work starts with worship and teaching, because people grow when they are fed. It continues in homes, schools and clinics, because faith that never leaves the building has not finished its work.

We believe every person carries gifts from God that the world needs. Our part is to help draw those gifts out, to wipe tears where there is pain, and to protect the dignity of everyone who walks through our doors, whatever their tribe, colour or nationality.

The ministry is registered as an incorporated trustee and governed by a written constitution. That means clear leadership, open books and accountability to the people we serve.

Narration

Stewardship

How every naira is handled.

Trust is earned in the details. These rules are written into the constitution we filed with the Corporate Affairs Commission.

Article 10

Our own account

All money is paid into bank accounts in the ministry's name as soon as it is received. Cash on hand is kept under ₦50,000.

Article 10

Two signatures

The Chairman and the Treasurer sign together. No single person can move the ministry's money alone.

Article 11

Audited every year

Licensed, independent auditors review our records yearly. The audited accounts are filed with the Commission.

Article 13

No private profit

Income and property serve the mission only. Nothing is paid out to members as dividend, bonus or profit.
